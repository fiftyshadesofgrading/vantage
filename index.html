Cape Ann · Portland, Maine · work in progress

Stand where they stood.

Fitz Henry Lane, Winslow Homer, Childe Hassam, Edward Hopper and the old postcard photographers all set up on these shores. Vantage pins their pictures to the spot, then lets you hold your phone up and see the painting laid over the real view.

How it works

Pick a picture

Every pin marks where an artist or photographer stood. The shaded cone shows which way they looked.

Walk there

Follow the distance readout to the spot. Pins are marked documented, inferred or general, so you know how sure we are.

Ghost it

Open the camera and the painting floats over the scene. Fade or swipe between then and now, and save the pair.

Free, and staying free

Vantage is a personal project. There's nothing to buy and no account, and it doesn't track you. Your position stays on your phone.
